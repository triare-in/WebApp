## Before you publish
- Indian e-commerce rules expect clear, accurate information on **returns, refunds, exchange, warranty, delivery and shipment, cost of return shipping, payment methods and grievance redressal**, plus the name, designation and contact details of a **grievance officer** shown on the site. Complaints should be acknowledged within 48 hours and resolved within a month. (Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020; verify the current text.)
- A "no returns" rule is allowed to be stated, but it must be **visible before purchase** (this is why checkout has an acknowledgement checkbox), accurate, and must not remove rights consumers have by law (for example for goods that are defective or not as described). Ask your lawyer where the line sits.
- Razorpay's live-account review normally looks for shipping, refund/cancellation, privacy and terms pages and real contact details. Check their current checklist.

## 2. Returns, cancellation and refunds
*Last updated: [[Effective date]]*

**In short:** Every keyboard is custom-built for you. **We do not accept returns of builds, and we do not offer replacements of whole builds.** If a part is faulty or damaged we can send a **replacement part free of charge**. A **refund** is only possible when the product is **returned to us at your expense** and the claim is approved.

### 2.1 Cancelling an order
- You can cancel until your order moves to *Building* [[NOTE: confirm the cut-off]]. Contact [[Support email]] with your order ID.
- After building starts, an order can't be cancelled because the parts are made for you.
- Approved cancellations are refunded to the original payment method within [[5–7]] business days.

### 2.2 No returns or exchanges of builds
We don't accept returns or exchanges for change of mind, colour or layout preference, compatibility choices you made in the configurator, or parts you supplied yourself (for example your own switches or keycaps).

### 2.3 Replacement parts (free, with proof)
If a part arrives faulty, damaged, or stops working, raise a claim from **Support → Raise a claim** with:
1. your order ID,
2. a clear description, and
3. **genuine proof**: clear photos or a short video showing the problem [[NOTE: add "and the outer packaging if damaged in transit"]].

If the proof is genuine, we ship the replacement part **free of charge**, including shipping. We may ask you to send back the faulty part [[NOTE: decide yes/no and who pays]].

### 2.4 Refunds
A refund is considered **only** if all of these are true:
- you raised a claim within **[[7]] days of delivery** [[NOTE: choose the window]],
- the reason is one we accept: [[NOTE: choose and list, for example "the product is materially different from what you ordered" or "the product is defective on arrival and can't be fixed with replacement parts"]],
- the claim is **approved** and we've given you a **claim number and return address**, and
- the product is **returned to us at your own expense** to: **[[Return address]]**.

**Process**
1. Raise a claim. We reply within **[[2]] business days**.
2. If approved, we send you the return address.
3. Ship the product within **[[7]] days** using a trackable courier. You pay the return shipping and are responsible for the parcel until we receive it. Pack it securely.
4. We inspect it within **[[3]] business days** of receipt.
5. If it matches the claim, we refund the amount paid [[NOTE: decide whether original shipping is refunded]] to the original payment method within **[[5–7]] business days**.

Refunds are not issued for products that arrive damaged because of poor packaging by you, show misuse or unauthorised modification, or are returned without an approved claim.

### 2.5 Your statutory rights
Nothing in this policy limits any rights you have under applicable consumer law. [[NOTE: lawyer to confirm wording.]]

## 3. Warranty and replacement parts
*Last updated: [[Effective date]]*

**Coverage period.** Products marked **"1-year warranty"** on their page and on your order are covered for **12 months from the delivery date** [[NOTE: or invoice date]]. Items without a warranty badge are not covered by this warranty. Your order page shows each item's warranty status and end date.

**What's covered.** Manufacturing defects and failures under normal use of covered products, such as a dead socket, a faulty controller, or a battery that fails prematurely [[NOTE: define "premature" for batteries]].

**What we do.** If your claim is approved, we send a **free replacement part** (including shipping) or, if a part can't be replaced, [[NOTE: repair / alternative remedy]].

**What's not covered**
- Damage from drops, liquids, excessive force, or improper assembly
- Wear and tear (for example keycap shine, switch wear, gradual battery capacity loss)
- Third-party parts you supplied or installed, and damage they cause
- Modifications beyond the swaps described in our repair pages [[NOTE: and firmware not supplied or recommended by us, if you decide to exclude it]]
- Cosmetic marks that don't affect function
- Products no longer within the coverage period

**How to claim.** Sign in, open **Support → Raise a claim**, choose your order, describe the issue and upload proof. We reply within [[2]] business days. Claims are tracked with a claim number on your account.

**Free replacement parts.** Approved warranty parts ship free. Genuine proof is required.
