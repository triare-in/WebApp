Privacy

Effective 01 September 2026 · Harness, Bengaluru

What we collect

Your account details — name, email, and the address you give us at checkout — along with the builds you save or share while customizing.

Your orders and payments, processed through Razorpay. We never store your card numbers; payment stays with the provider.

How we use it

To build and ship your keyboard, and to send the order updates you signed up for at checkout.

To improve layouts, pricing, and the configurator. We don't use your build for marketing without asking.

What we never do

We don't sell or rent your data, and we don't turn a build you save into an ad or testimonial without your say-so.

We don't keep personal data past account deletion. Delete your account and the record goes with it.

Your controls

Export or delete your data any time from Account → Data. You can also ask us to do it by email.

One email reply is all it takes — no forms, no back-and-forth. Contact support if anything is unclear.

Shipping

Standard shipping is ₹150 and takes 10–14 days because keyboards are built to order. Express is ₹399 for 5–7 days. Cash on delivery isn't available.

Returns and refunds

Stock, unbuilt keyboards can be returned within 14 days if undamaged. Custom builds are covered by the 2-year warranty instead — we repair, swap, or replace the part.
