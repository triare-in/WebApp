About

We got tired of keyboards that die quietly.

Most mechanical keyboards are sealed shut, glued together, and gone in a year or two. We build one that you can open, and rebuild it for as long as you type.

Why repairable

One keyboard, four parts, no glue. When a part wears out — a switch, a hot-swap socket, a battery — you buy the part, not the product. We document every teardown and sell the pieces by name, so the day a key stops feeling right, you're ten minutes and one screwdriver away.

Why wireless-first

ZMK open firmware, three paired devices, and a USB-C port when you want the cable. Set your bindings once and the keyboard remembers them across a laptop, a phone, and a desktop. Nothing is locked to a vendor — you can flash it yourself, and the source is public.

Why India

Built and tested in Bengaluru, shipped across India in 10–14 days, and supported in your time zone. Real people answer, and the warranty is measured in years, not months. When you write to us, you're writing to the person who packed your box.

So far, Harness is one person, one workbench, and a lot of pulled sockets.
